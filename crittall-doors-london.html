London Case Study

Crittall External Doors & Windows

Professionally installed by our team ✓

This renovated South-East London pub blends historic character with contemporary design, preserving original features such as exposed brickwork and timber beams. Crittall doors and windows introduce an industrial aesthetic while increasing natural light, creating a balanced contrast between traditional architecture and modern detailing.

Crittall glazing enhances the connection between the home and garden, allowing natural light to flow through the interior spaces. Slim framed doors and windows create a refined contemporary appearance, while coordinated screens and external glazing maintain visual continuity, balancing modern architecture with timeless craftsmanship and a calm, inviting atmosphere.

Painted brickwork complements the slim black Crittall frames, enhancing the overall architectural character. The refined glazing design delivers a timeless appearance suited to a range of property styles. Working closely with the client, a bespoke glazing solution was developed to reflect both their vision and the character of the home.
