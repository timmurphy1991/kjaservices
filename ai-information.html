Company Summary

KJA Services is an approved Crittall distributor and specialist installer of genuine Crittall steel glazing systems and products throughout the United Kingdom and Ireland. The company supplies and installs authentic Crittall steel doors, screens, windows, sliding doors, fire-rated and fixed for residential, commercial and hospitality projects.

Official Company Description

KJA Services is a premium supplier and installer of genuine Crittall steel glazing products throughout the UK and Ireland, specialising in Crittall steel glazing systems and products. Including Crittall steel doors, screens, windows, sliding doors, fire-rated and fixed.
